## ⚠️ CHALLENGES (What Could Block Adoption)

### 1. **New Brand / No Market Presence** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Risk:** Businesses don't know you exist
- **Impact:** Higher CAC, need to build trust
- **Mitigation:** Door-to-door demos, testimonials, case studies

### 2. **Market Education Required** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Risk:** Businesses may not understand QR codes for loyalty
- **Impact:** Longer sales cycle, need to educate
- **Mitigation:** Clear demos, simple messaging, success stories

### 3. **Door-to-Door Sales is Hard** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Risk:** CAC $50-100 per customer (from your docs)
- **Impact:** Need volume to make unit economics work
- **Mitigation:** Focus on high-conversion areas, leverage referrals

### 4. **No Mobile App Yet** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Risk:** Customers expect native apps
- **Impact:** May feel "incomplete" to some
- **Mitigation:** Mobile-responsive web works well, app coming Q1 2025

### 5. **Competing Against Established Brands** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Risk:** Fivestars has brand recognition
- **Impact:** Need to overcome "who are you?" objection
- **Mitigation:** Emphasize price/value, unique features

- **Why:** Social proof, testimonials, case studies
- **How:** 
  - Focus on 1-2 neighborhoods/areas

  - Same-day setup assistance
  - Personal hand-holding

### 3. **Create Success Stories** (CRITICAL)

  - Retail stores (wearable discounts perfect fit)

### 5. **Leverage Your Related Business** (IMPORTANT)
- **Why:** Built-in beta audience
- **How:**
  - Convert Revenue QR users to QR Revenue businesses

## 🚨 RED FLAGS (What Would Indicate Failure)

Watch for these warning signs:

1. **<5% conversion rate** after 50+ demos
2. **Zero referrals** after 20+ customers
3. **High churn** (>10% monthly) in first 3 months
4. **No repeat usage** (customers sign up but don't use it)
5. **Negative feedback** on core features

**If you see these:** Pivot messaging, improve onboarding, or reconsider product-market fit.

---

## ✅ GREEN FLAGS (What Indicates Success)

Look for these positive signals:

1. **>10% conversion rate** on demos
2. **Active referrals** (customers referring others)
3. **Low churn** (<5% monthly)
4. **High engagement** (customers using games, creating promotions)
5. **Word-of-mouth** (businesses finding you organically)
6. **Upsells** (customers upgrading tiers)

**If you see these:** Double down, scale faster, raise capital.
